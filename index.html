Saturday in Denver

Commencement is Sunday morning. Here are some ways to enjoy Saturday before the 6:30 PM dinner.

Morning · 10 – 12

Denver Botanic Gardens

Flat paths, plenty of benches, shade. Then a scenic drive through Congress Park & Cherry Creek neighborhoods.

Lunch · 12 – 1:30

Cherry Creek

Eat near Cherry Creek Shopping Center. Plenty of seating and restrooms. Relax while others browse.

Afternoon · 1:30 – 3:30

Union Station

Coffee or a drink in the main hall. Optional strolls to nearby LoDo shops. Great people-watching spot.

Late Afternoon · 3:30 – 5

Rest at Hotel

Naps, showers, regroup before the 6:30 dinner.

All stops have paved surfaces, benches, restrooms, and easy drop-offs. Trim to just Botanic Gardens + Union Station if energy is low.

Morning · 9:30 – 12

Red Rocks Park & Amphitheatre

25–30 min drive. Park near the upper lot for less stairs. Incredible vistas from the amphitheatre and visitor center.

Lunch · 12 – 1:30

Morrison or Downtown Denver

Small walkable main street in Morrison, or head back to Denver for more options.

Afternoon · 1:30 – 3:30

RiNo Art District

Colorful murals, Denver Central Market for snacks. Camp at a table while others explore.

Late Afternoon · 3:30 – 5

Hotel Downtime

Rest and reset before the dinner reservation.

Red Rocks is dramatic but adjustable for different mobility levels. RiNo adds a younger, urban, artsy vibe.
